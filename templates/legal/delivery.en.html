Delivery is added to the order total and shown at checkout before you pay.

2. To a post office

At checkout you choose your region and district, from the list or on the map. The order goes by Uzpost’s Bir Qadam service to the Bir Qadam office of that district. Where a district has more than one, Uzpost decides which the parcel goes to. The office’s address appears on the order page once the parcel is on its way.

Take your passport or ID card with you: the parcel is handed to the recipient named on the order.

3. To your door

An Uzpost courier brings the order to the address you gave and calls ahead. You can type the address or mark it on the map — the pin makes it more precise, but the street and house number are still needed.

4. How long it takes

Once your payment is confirmed, we pack the order and hand it to Uzpost. The parcel’s number and where it is now are shown on the order page. How long each method usually takes is shown above, next to its price. In any case your order must arrive no later than {{ legal.delivery_deadline_days }} day{{ legal.delivery_deadline_days|pluralize }} after payment.

5. If you don’t collect it

The office holds a Bir Qadam parcel for {{ legal.hold_days }} day{{ legal.hold_days|pluralize }}. Collect it soon after it arrives.

A parcel that is not collected in time, or is refused, comes back to us. That costs you nothing: your payment is refunded in full within {{ legal.refund_days }} day{{ legal.refund_days|pluralize }}. Even so, check the district and the address carefully — you can see them again on the order page.

6. If the size is wrong

We exchange an unworn, unwashed item with its labels on for another size we have, free of charge, within {{ legal.exchange_days }} day{{ legal.exchange_days|pluralize }} of buying it: we pay for sending the new size. If the size you need has sold out, we refund you. If an item is returned after it has been collected because of its size or a change of mind, the buyer pays for delivery.

Send a request on the site or on Telegram — we will tell you the next steps and where to send the item.

7. If it is faulty or not what you ordered

If the item arrives faulty or damaged, or you were sent the wrong size, colour or model, write to us as soon as you can with a photo or video: we will replace it or refund it, and we pay all the delivery costs. You can report a defect within {{ legal.defect_claim_months }} month{{ legal.defect_claim_months|pluralize }} of receiving the item.

If the packaging is damaged, inspect the parcel at the branch and have a report drawn up in the presence of a postal employee.

The full rules are in the Returns and exchange policy and the Terms of use.
