This document is published in Uzbek, Russian and English. If the versions differ, the Uzbek text prevails.

We want you to be happy with what you buy from GRAPHIX. If a size doesn’t fit, you find a fault, or you want to return or exchange something for another reason, we’re here to help.

1. Time limit for returns and exchanges

You can ask to return or exchange an item on the conditions below within {{ legal.exchange_days }} days of the day you bought it.

Any other time limits and consumer rights provided by law still apply.

2. Conditions for returns and exchanges

To return or exchange an item of proper quality, these conditions must be met:

  • The item is unused and unwashed.
  • It is undamaged, with no stains or odours.
  • Its original labels and packaging are kept.
  • Its original appearance and properties are preserved.
  • There is an order number or other proof of purchase.

Trying an item on only to check the size does not, by itself, stop it from being returned.

3. Exchanges

If an item you bought doesn’t fit, you can exchange it free of charge for another size we have.

  • If the size you need is available, the item is exchanged.
  • GRAPHIX pays for delivering the new item.
  • If the size you need is not available, you may choose a refund in accordance with the law.

If a parcel comes back without being collected at the Uzpost office, the return is free. If an item is returned after it has been collected because of its size or a change of mind, the buyer pays for delivery, unless the law provides otherwise.

4. Faulty or wrong items

If you received an item that is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, get in touch with us.

This includes:

  • The wrong size, colour or model was sent.
  • A manufacturing fault in the fabric, the seams or the print.
  • The item does not match its description on the site.

In such cases you may, in accordance with the law, ask for a replacement, a refund or another appropriate remedy.

GRAPHIX pays the costs of returns and exchanges caused by its own mistake or by a fault in the item.

Your legal rights for a faulty item are not limited to the ordinary {{ legal.exchange_days }}-day exchange period.

5. Refunds

If a request to return an item and have your money back is in line with the law, the amount paid is refunded.

  • Unless agreed otherwise, the money is returned by the method you paid with.
  • Refunds are made within the periods set by law.
  • A returned item may be inspected.
  • Delivery charges and other payments are accounted for in accordance with the law in force.

6. Made-to-order items

Some GRAPHIX items may be made, or have their print applied, after the order is accepted.

For designs from our standard catalogue, being made after the order does not by itself take away the right to a return or exchange.

Returns and exchanges of items made individually at a customer’s personal request are considered in accordance with the law in force.

7. How to get in touch

To return or exchange an item, choose one of these ways:

  • On GRAPHIX.UZ: send a request from the returns and exchanges section of the site.
  • On Telegram: write to our support.

In your request, include:

  • The order number.
  • The item you’re returning or exchanging.
  • The reason.
  • Photos of the item, where needed.

Once we’ve looked at your request, we’ll tell you the next steps and where to send the item.

8. Final provisions

This policy applies in accordance with the consumer protection law of the Republic of Uzbekistan in force.

Nothing in this policy limits a consumer’s rights guaranteed by law.

If you have any questions, contact GRAPHIX support.
