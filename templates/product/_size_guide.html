{% load i18n i18n_fields %} {% comment %} The size guide, drawn by the site (§9 Phase 23 part C, §17 #400). One partial for `/olcham-jadvali/` and for the product page's size viewer, so the two cannot drift apart. Expects `charts`, the charts to show, each with rows and its rows' sizes fetched; and `size_fit`, the rows product.size_charts.fit_rows() makes. The product page passes its product's own chart alone (item 18). A drawing of the shirt with the four measures numbered on it and no words inside it; under it one table, the sizes down and the measures across, each heading carrying the number it has on the drawing; then the table for choosing a size. Every word is in the page's language, and each figure is said once: the picture that stood here said them in three languages at once and the page then said them again. The drawing is a T-shirt laid flat. The two short curves are the seams the sleeves hang from: the shoulder is measured between them and the sleeve from one of them down. Its sizes are the drawing's own geometry, in its viewBox, so they scale with it; its colours are the site's tokens. It is hidden from a screen reader, which has the same measures named in the table's headings. {% endcomment %}
