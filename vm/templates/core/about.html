Our story

A studio,
not a factory.

VM Graphics started out of a single room with one sewing machine and a screen press. The brief was simple: make clothes the way our friends wanted to wear them, in our own city, without compromise.

Every piece you see here is designed, cut, printed and finished in-house, in small batches, by people whose names we know.
