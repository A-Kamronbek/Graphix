2. What we collect

Your account

Username, first name (optional), phone number and whether it has been confirmed, password, and when the account was created and last signed into. The password is stored only as a one-way hash — nobody, including us, can read it.

Orders

The recipient’s full name and phone number; the region and district or city; the post office postcode or the street and house number; the coordinates of the pin, if you drop one on the map; your note to the order; the products, prices, delivery method and charge, payment method, the order’s status and the history of its changes.

Payment

When you pay with Click, we receive only the order number, the amount and whether the payment went through. Your card number and other card details are entered with Click and never reach us.

Reviews and messages

Your review (rating, text, photos) and the order it belongs to; a message you send through the contact form (subject and text) together with the phone number on your account.

Cart and saved designs

The products in your cart and the designs you have saved with the heart. If you are not signed in, your cart is linked to a session in your browser.

Technical data

The web server’s logs record your IP address, browser and device type, the page opened and the time. Your IP address is also used to limit repeated attempts such as password guessing; for that it is kept as a one-way hash for up to {{ legal.rate_limit_minutes }} minute{{ legal.rate_limit_minutes|pluralize }}.

We use no advertising or analytics tracking (no analytics counters, no ad pixels, nothing of the kind).

3. Why we use it

  • Your account — confirming your phone number, resetting your password by SMS code, keeping the account secure;
  • Fulfilling orders — checkout, taking payment, preparing the parcel and handing it to Uzpost, contacting you;
  • Customer service — answering your questions, handling exchange and return requests;
  • Reviews — checking and publishing them on the Site;
  • Security — preventing fraud and abuse;
  • Legal duties — keeping accounting and tax records.

We process data on the basis of your consent (given when you register), of performing a contract you are party to, and of the Seller’s obligations under the law.

We never send marketing SMS. The only messages your phone receives from us are service messages — confirmation codes.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or pass it to anyone for advertising. It goes only to the services below, and only as much of it as each needs to do its job:

  • Eskiz (eskiz.uz) — SMS delivery: your phone number and the text of the confirmation code;
  • Click (click.uz) — payments: the order number and amount; Click takes your card details itself;
  • Uzpost (Oʻzbekiston pochtasi JSC) — delivery: the recipient’s full name, phone number and address or post office;
  • Google — Google Maps: when the map is switched on, opening the checkout page makes your browser load Google’s script, and Google receives your IP address and technical details about your browser. If you drop a pin, its coordinates are sent to Google to look up the address;
  • Telegram — notices of new orders, payments, messages and reviews are sent to the store’s private Telegram chat. An order notice contains the recipient’s name, phone number, address, the items and the order note;
  • Our hosting provider — the Site and its database run on a rented server.

Where the law requires it, data may be disclosed at the request of the competent public authorities.

5. Transfers outside Uzbekistan

Google’s and Telegram’s servers are outside Uzbekistan, so sending data to them is a cross-border transfer. It is carried out in line with the Law, including on the basis of your consent: by registering and accepting this policy, you also consent to these transfers.

We do not collect biometric or genetic data.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The Site uses only the cookies it cannot work without:

  • sessionid — remembers that you are signed in, and holds your cart if you are not; lasts {{ legal.session_days }} day{{ legal.session_days|pluralize }};
  • csrftoken — protects forms against forged requests; up to one year;
  • graphix_lang — remembers the language you chose; one year;
  • messages — carries a one-off notice such as “Added to cart” to the next page and disappears once it has been shown.

Your browser’s temporary storage (sessionStorage) holds two drafts: the sign-up form without the password, so it is not lost while you read the terms, and the checkout form, while you look at the post office map. This storage is cleared when you close the tab and is never sent to us.

No advertising or tracking cookies are used. When the Google map loads, Google may collect technical data under its own rules — see Google’s privacy policy.

You can turn cookies off in your browser settings, but signing in and the cart will then stop working.

9. How we protect it

Your connection to the Site is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Only authorised staff can reach the database and the order management panel. Sign-in and code attempts are limited. Location data is removed from review photos.

If a data breach happens, we will tell you as soon as we can and, where the law requires it, the competent authority.
